abstract: Node-level random walk has been widely used to improve Graph Neural Networks.
  However, there is limited attention to random walk on edge and, more generally,
  on $k$-simplices. This paper systematically analyzes how random walk on different
  orders of simplicial complexes (SC) facilitates GNNs in their theoretical expressivity.
  First, on $0$-simplices or node level, we establish a connection between existing
  positional encoding (PE) and structure encoding (SE) methods through the bridge
  of random walk. Second, on $1$-simplices or edge level, we bridge edge-level random
  walk and Hodge $1$-Laplacians and design corresponding edge PE respectively. In
  the spatial domain, we directly make use of edge level random walk to construct
  EdgeRWSE. Based on the spectral analysis of Hodge $1$-Laplcians, we propose Hodge1Lap,
  a permutation equivariant and expressive edge-level positional encoding. Third,
  we generalize our theory to random walk on higher-order simplices and propose the
  general principle to design PE on simplices based on random walk and Hodge Laplacians.
  Inter-level random walk is also introduced to unify a wide range of simplicial networks.
  Extensive experiments verify the effectiveness of our random walk-based methods.
