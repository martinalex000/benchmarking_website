---
title: "Benchmark Groups"
description: "Overview of benchmark groups, their introduction papers, and aggregate dataset/result coverage."
---

Benchmark groups are the sets of benchmarks that have been introduced together, e.g. the 5 LRGB datasets, all the OGB datasets, or the more recent GraphBench suite of benchmarks. Ordering by number of results reported (normalized by number of datasets) in the last two years.
